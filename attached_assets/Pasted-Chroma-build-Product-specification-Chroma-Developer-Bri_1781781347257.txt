Chroma Developer Brief

Build Specification for Replit Vibe Coding

Product Name

Chroma

Tagline

The professional filmmaker platform.

Vision

Build a premium web platform that combines professional video hosting, monetisation, and filmmaker networking into a single ecosystem.

Chroma should become the professional identity platform for filmmakers, replacing fragmented tools such as Vimeo, LinkedIn, YouTube portfolios, Facebook groups, production forums, and screener services.

The platform must feel premium, creative, cinematic, and industry-focused rather than social-media-focused.

⸻

Core User Types

Directors

Upload work, showcase portfolios, find crew, hire collaborators.

Cinematographers

Display reels, discover projects, receive endorsements.

Editors

Showcase edits, collaborate with filmmakers.

Producers

Manage projects, recruit talent.

Composers & Sound Designers

Present work and connect with productions.

Film Students

Build profiles, learn, receive feedback, discover opportunities.

Production Companies

Host work professionally and recruit talent.

Film Schools

Manage student showcases and communities.

⸻

MVP Goal

Launch a functioning platform that proves three core pillars:

1. Professional Video Hosting
2. Professional Networking
3. Career Discovery

Monetisation, DRM, advanced streaming, and NLE integrations can be phased in later.

Success Metrics

MVP is successful when:

* Users can create profiles
* Users can upload videos
* Users can discover filmmakers
* Users can follow creators
* Users can send messages
* Users can post opportunities

Build for quality and simplicity first. Advanced streaming and enterprise features come later.
