Chroma

Professional Filmmaker Platform

Product Overview

Chroma is a professional filmmaking platform designed for filmmakers, production companies, cinematographers, editors, colourists, sound designers, and creative professionals.

Unlike consumer video platforms, Chroma is not advertising-driven. Its purpose is to provide filmmakers with tools to showcase portfolios, host media assets, distribute films, collaborate with clients, monetise content, and manage professional digital storage.

The platform combines:

* Professional portfolio hosting
* Video hosting and streaming
* Media asset management
* Cloud storage
* Video-on-demand distribution
* Digital product sales
* Live event streaming
* Cinema-on-demand screenings
* Public filmmaker profiles

Strategic Direction

Chroma should be built around two distinct business pillars.

Pillar 1: Portfolio Hosting + Cloud Storage

Benefits:

* Recurring subscription revenue
* Predictable cash flow
* Lower legal complexity
* Easier support requirements
* Faster MVP delivery

Pillar 2: Film Distribution + Chroma Cinema

Benefits:

* Higher revenue potential
* Creator monetisation
* Premium positioning

Challenges:

* Copyright disputes
* Refunds
* Tax compliance
* Rights verification
* Customer support
* Payment management

Recommendation:

Launch with Portfolio Hosting + Cloud Storage first, then introduce film sales, rentals, and Chroma Cinema after the platform has established recurring subscription revenue.
